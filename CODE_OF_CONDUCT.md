# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

COSMIC Widget contributors and participants are expected to maintain a
respectful, constructive, and harassment-free environment.

Positive behavior includes:

- Giving and accepting technical feedback in good faith.
- Focusing criticism on the work rather than the person.
- Respecting differing experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or bad-faith participation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact options on the [ZlordHUN GitHub profile](https://github.com/ZlordHUN).
Do not disclose sensitive reports in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Reports will
be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter is not permitted.
